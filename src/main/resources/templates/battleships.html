Battleships

How to Play

1. Place your 3-cell ship.

2. Choose Horizontal or Vertical.

3. Click a square to place your ship.

4. Place your 2-cell ship.

5. Click squares on the Computer Board to fire.

6. Sink both enemy ships before the computer sinks yours!
